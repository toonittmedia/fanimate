**Fanimate 0.2.2** is a big usability release. You can keep several projects open in tabs, projects are now single files, layers have selection handles and a pivot, there are nine new drawing and editing tools, and the canvas has rulers, guides, a grid and snapping. It is still an alpha: keep backups, and please [report what goes wrong](https://github.com/toonittmedia/fanimate/issues).

## Before you update

- **Projects are saved as one file now.** Older folder projects still open and stay folders when you save them. A project saved by 0.2.2 cannot be opened by 0.2.1 or earlier (the file format is now version 4), so keep a copy if you need to go back.
- **Add Bone moved to Alt+M** in the default (Fanimate) keymap, because Shift+M now belongs to Shape Builder. The Moho keymap is unchanged for bones.

## New in this release

### Tabs and a Home screen

Open **several projects at once**, each in its own tab under the title bar. New, Open and the samples add a tab. Close with the `x` or Ctrl+W, reorder by dragging, switch with Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab. A dot marks unsaved changes. Every tab has its own undo history, frame, canvas view and timeline zoom.

The pinned **Home tab** gives you New file, Open, Import, six size presets, your recent projects and the three sample templates. Choose what opens at startup (Home, your last session, a blank file, or your most recent file) in Preferences or on the Home tab.

Opening a `.fnm` from your file manager hands it to the Fanimate that is already running, as a new tab.

If Fanimate crashes with unsaved work in several tabs, it offers to bring each one back.

### One file per project

A `.fnm` project is now a single file (a ZIP of the old folder layout, with a checksum for every part). Saving is atomic: Fanimate writes a new file beside the old one, reads it back to check it, then swaps it in, so a failed save leaves your last good file alone. We tested this by killing the process at thousands of random moments, with no corrupt files.

The installers register the file type, so your file manager shows the Fanimate icon and opens projects with a double-click.

### Selection handles, pivot and corner radius

- **Transform Layer** draws a box around the selection in the layer's label colour. Corner and edge handles scale; the round button below the box, or the area just outside a corner, rotates. Shift keeps proportions or snaps rotation to 15 degrees; Alt scales from the centre. The size or angle shows beside the pointer while you drag.
- **Pivot:** every layer can have a pivot, shown as a ring you can drag (it snaps to the nine anchors of the box) or set with the 3 x 3 grid and X and Y fields in Properties. Rotation and scaling use it. Old projects without a pivot look exactly the same as before.
- **Select Points** now selects a whole shape when you click its fill or stroke, a single point when you click a point, and moves a shape when you drag its body. A selection box picks every shape it touches (Alt: only the points inside). The shape under the pointer gets a thin grey outline.
- **Rectangles** remember they are rectangles: drag the round dots in the corners, or type a **Corner radius** in Properties (Absolute or Relative). **Expand Shape** turns one into a plain path. Editing a point by hand does the same.
- A **floating toolbar** appears below the selection with the actions that fit it: Edit Points or Transform, Group, Unite, Expand Shape, fill and stroke colours, Duplicate, Delete and a menu of layer commands.

### New drawing and editing tools

The Fanimate keymap is shown below; the Moho keymap uses different letters where Moho has a tool of that kind, and every shortcut shows in the tool's tooltip.

- **Curvature (C):** click to add smooth points along a curve, Alt+click for a corner, Esc to finish. A ghost segment previews the next piece.
- **Line (L), Polygon (Shift+L), Star (Shift+O), Triangle (Shift+R):** drag to draw. The mouse wheel changes the number of sides or points while you drag; Alt changes a star's inner radius.
- **Freehand with Width (Shift+B):** draw brush strokes whose width you set as you go.
- **Delete Edge (Shift+E):** click a segment to remove it and split the shape.
- **Magnet (Shift+G):** drag near points to pull them toward the pointer; Alt pushes them away. The draw width sets the reach.
- **Scale Points (Shift+Q)** and **Rotate Points (Shift+A):** scale or turn the selected points around their centre; Shift snaps turns to 15 degrees.
- **Draw Shape (Shift+K):** click a shape to pick up its fill and stroke colour for new artwork.
- **Line Width (Shift+I):** drag along a stroke to widen or narrow it.
- **Shape Builder (Shift+M):** drag across overlapping shapes to unite them; Alt+drag subtracts.

### Rulers, guides, grid and snapping

- **Rulers** run along the top and left of the canvas, with a line that follows the pointer. The corner button cycles px, pt, mm and in. Toggle them in **View > Rulers**.
- **Guides:** drag from a ruler to place one. Right-click a guide to delete or lock it.
- **Grid:** **View > Grid** shows a configurable grid under your artwork. It hides itself when zoomed out far enough to turn into noise.
- **Snapping** (View > Snapping): pixels, grid, guides and the canvas edges and centre, with a hint line showing what you snapped to. Moving layers and points, and drawing with the pen, curve, shape and bone tools, all snap. Hold Shift while adding a bone to snap its angle to the step you set (15 degrees by default).

### Bones panel

**Window > Bones** (docked in the Rig workspace) lists the whole skeleton indented by parent. Drag a bone to reorder it or move it under another bone, double-click to rename, and edit its parent, length, pin, angle limits and strength without leaving the panel. A rig health summary at the bottom lists anything wrong.

### Style panel

Shapes in the Style panel now have a grip: drag it to change the drawing order, while fill, stroke colour and stroke width stay editable on every row.

### Timeline and zoom

- The ruler and rows continue past the end frame, dimmed. The start and end of the playing range are marked, and the range is shaded.
- The transport bar reads "Frame N of M", and M is an editable field.
- Zoom in, zoom out, reset and fit-all buttons sit in the timeline header. Ctrl+scroll and the buttons keep the playhead where it is on screen.
- Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus and Ctrl+0 no longer scale the whole interface. They zoom the canvas, or the timeline when the pointer is over it.
- Menus, tooltips and panel dividers are flatter: 1 px borders and almost no shadows.

## Not there yet

- **Stroke Exposure** (trim the start or end of a stroke) is listed in the toolbox but disabled. It needs a new field in the file format, so it will come in a later release.
- **Snap to shapes** (the points, edges and centres of other artwork) is not built.
- The playhead still stops at the end frame, so you cannot add keys beyond it by moving there.
- "Your files" on the Home tab, templates beyond the three samples, and separate radii for each corner are not built.
- The floating toolbar cannot be dragged, and has no Align, Offset Path or Lock yet.
- On macOS, opening a file from Finder into an already running Fanimate has not been tested.

## Behind the scenes

- File format version 4: layers have a `pivot`, rectangles store `rect`, and guides are saved in `document.json`. Files from every earlier version still open; older readers refuse version 4 files. The specification is in [`docs/spec/fnm.md`](https://github.com/toonittmedia/fanimate/blob/main/docs/spec/fnm.md).
- The ZIP reader and writer live in `fanimate-fnm` and are shared with the importers. The damaged-projects test now also damages 1,500 single-file projects and checks that none can crash the app.
- The Bones panel, the snapping module and the new editing tools are covered by unit tests that run without a window.
